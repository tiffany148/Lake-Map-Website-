More than one creative lane

I'm a songwriter, maker, lake-life enthusiast, and curious collector of ideas. I love turning the things I care about — family, nature, music, crystals, and everyday chaos — into things other people can enjoy, use, or remember.

Life is better at the lake

Greers Ferry Lake is home base and a constant source of inspiration. Around here, the best ideas tend to show up somewhere between a boat ride, a sunset, and one more project I swear I'll finish tonight. Finished is better than flawless.
